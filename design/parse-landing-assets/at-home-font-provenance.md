# At home option fonts: source and license

Downloaded 2026-10-02 from official Google Fonts endpoints as self-hosted Latin WOFF2 files. The returned files have the WOFF2 `wOF2` signature and Google CSS declares the requested weights.

- **DM Serif Display**, regular 400: `frontend/public/landing/fonts/dm-serif-display-latin-regular.woff2` (24,744 bytes). Source: https://fonts.gstatic.com/s/dmserifdisplay/v17/-nFnOHM81r4j6k0gjAW3mujVU2B2G_Bx0g.woff2 . Google Fonts CSS: https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=DM+Serif+Display&family=DM+Sans:wght@400..700&display=swap .
- **DM Sans**, variable 400–700: `frontend/public/landing/fonts/dm-sans-latin-variable.woff2` (36,932 bytes). Source: https://fonts.gstatic.com/s/dmsans/v17/rP2Yp2ywxg089UriI5-g4vlH9VoD8Cmcqbu0-K4.woff2 . Google Fonts CSS above declares `font-weight: 400 700` for this Latin file.

Both typefaces use the SIL Open Font License 1.1. License texts from the official Google Fonts repository are preserved at `frontend/public/landing/fonts/OFL-DM-Serif-Display.txt` and `frontend/public/landing/fonts/OFL-DM-Sans.txt`; source pages: https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/dmserifdisplay/OFL.txt and https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/dmsans/OFL.txt .
