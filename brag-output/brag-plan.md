# Brag Plan: Parse

## Planning rubric
1. Parse helps older adults record care updates, check the read-back, and share confirmed records with permitted caregivers.
2. Strongest claim: “Care, in your own words.” The meaningful distinction is that nothing is saved until the person confirms.
3. Visual hook: the real family's at-home photograph, paired with large DM Serif Display typography, then the Aurora Pearl companion interface.
4. Show the actual Talk to Parse action, review read-back, Confirm and save button, and a confirmed caregiver timeline note.
5. 22 seconds gives the care update room to be read and the confirmation room to register.
6. Polished; a warm, personal product film, calm motion and concise copy.
7. Steady warm music bed, three restrained interaction accents; narration disabled (no --voice).
8. “Care, in your own words. We built Parse so older adults can record an update, check it, and choose which caregivers can see it.”
9. Tap to record → review and confirm a care note → the permitted caregiver sees the confirmed record.

## The angle
A person's words become a useful care record, with their confirmation at the center. Show the current browser software rather than promising untested hardware behavior. Familiar, thoughtful, useful.

## Hook
“Care, in your own words.” over the repository's at-home family image. The title settles by 0.8s.

## Key moments
- Tap to record in the real companion's palette and typography.
- A short fictional note: “My knee felt stiff after our walk today.”
- Confirm and save produces a confirmed note with voice-note provenance in a permitted caregiver's timeline.

## Outro
“Your words. Your choice.” with the actual Parse logo and “Tencent Cloud Hackathon Singapore 2026 · Prototype”.

## User flow worth showing
Entry: Talk to Parse. Action: tap, then review the note and confirm. Result: confirmed care note is shared within granted caregiver access. UI is an animated recreation based on frontend/src/Companion.tsx and frontend/src/App.tsx; interaction and speech are simulated. Names and text are fictional stand-ins; no accounts, emails, endpoints, or private data appear.

## Storyboard
### Scene 1 — At home — 3.5s (0–3.5)
Real project family photo and “Care, in your own words.” Small Parse logo. Warm image drift; headline enters, then holds.
Sequential/interaction: none.
Audio intent: warm opening; music fades in.
Transition: gentle crossfade into companion.

### Scene 2 — Speak — 4.5s (3.5–8)
Left: “A moment to speak.” Right: enlarged companion panel, “Talk to Parse”, “Speak freely” and “Tap to record”. Cursor taps at 5.3s. Button changes to recording state and a short fictional spoken note appears as a large quote at 6.1s: “My knee felt stiff after our walk today.”
Sequential/interaction: simulated tap; note holds for at least 2.7s across the next transition.
Audio: subtle click on tap; music steady.
Transition: soft panel continuation into review.

### Scene 3 — Review — 6s (8–14)
Left: “Check the note before it’s saved.” Right: companion review recreation, “Please check this first”, “Care note”, same short read-back, “Confirm and save”, “Cancel”, “Hear read-back”. Cursor confirms at 12.55s. Confirmed and saved status appears; the quote has at least 3.5s settled reading time before the click.
Sequential/interaction: review content, simulated confirmation, receipt. Read-back and status remain readable.
Audio: warm reveal at 8.74s, quiet click/confirmation accent at 12.55s.
Transition: gentle slide/crossfade to caregiver.

### Scene 4 — Care circle and close — 8s (14–22)
14–18.5: caregiver timeline recreation with “Confirmed care note”, the same quote, “Source · Voice note”, and “Shared with permitted caregiver”. Left: “A clearer picture for your care circle.” Confirmation is visible before sharing result.
18.5–22: soft crossfade to project at-home photo, actual Parse logo, “Your words. Your choice.” and prototype event credit. End remains settled.
Sequential/interaction: single confirmed timeline entry reveal; then logo and closing copy.
Audio: sparse arrival accent; music fades from 20.7–22.

Music mood: steady and clean. Audio summary: restrained music supports the flow, with three small sounds for the actual UI actions.

Scene durations: 3.5 + 4.5 + 6 + 8 = 22 seconds.
