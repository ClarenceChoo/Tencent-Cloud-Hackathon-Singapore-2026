# Parse product demo

A captions-only, approximately 90-second walkthrough, rendered at 1920×1080 and 30 fps. No narration or music.

All footage is captured from the actual React/FastAPI app with a separate fictional database at ports 5174/8002. Existing software-demo data is untouched. The video demonstrates software available now; it does not demonstrate microphone capture, physical hardware, live AI, diagnosis or automatic dispensing.

1. **Parse** — Your everyday care record.
2. **See the day clearly** — Review medication reports, notes and readings in one timeline. Filter to the details you need.
3. **Keep track of medication** — Follow a checked plan and record what was reported. Reminders are separate from confirmed reports.
4. **Keep the details that matter** — Write a care note. Review and confirm it. Choose who can see it.
5. **Stay on top of follow-ups** — Move a care-plan task from due to arranged and record the appointment date.
6. **Ask for a little help** — Create a support request for a selected care member.
7. **Prepare for the next visit** — Review a source-linked summary, save the review, and open a read-only copy.
8. **Care, within reach** — Use the same timeline on a phone.
9. **Parse** — Notes. Routines. Follow-ups. One shared care record.
