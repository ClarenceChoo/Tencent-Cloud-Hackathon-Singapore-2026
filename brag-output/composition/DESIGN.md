# Parse — At home

## Style Prompt
A warm, polished film in the project's at-home and Aurora Pearl visual identities. Actual family photography, navy editorial serif headlines, mint interactive controls, large readable read-back. Typography communicates human speech and precise provenance. Simulated UI interactions remain specific to the source app.

## Colors
- Background #fbf8f2
- Foreground #1b2944
- Accent #176b78
- Mint #9cdddc
- Lavender #cbbfe8

## Typography
DM Serif Display (400) headlines and spoken quote; DM Sans (400/700) UI, source labels and event credit. Fonts are shipped locally.

## Constraints
No clinical outcomes, hardware claims, private data, visualizers, generic marketing statistics or abstract filler. UI has real app vocabulary and a fictional example. Transitions are 0.45-second crossfades. Long read-back holds; restrained audio. End on the logo and human photograph.
